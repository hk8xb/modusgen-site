From first call to production.

Four steps, each with a fixed fee and a written result. You can stop after any of them.

  1. 1

    Assessment

    1 to 2 weeks

    A baseline you sign, taken from your own invoices: which workloads can move, where each should run, and what you would save without us.

  2. 2

    Pilot

    4 weeks

    One environment, two model candidates, twenty developers and two repositories. Pass or fail against gates agreed before the start.

  3. 3

    Production

    4 to 6 weeks

    The platform, rollout to every developer, runbooks, acceptance tests, and ten business days of close support after go-live.

  4. 4

    Run

    Every month

    Updates, a model evaluation each quarter, and a savings report against the baseline you signed.

Durations are our plan. Your dates are set in the assessment.
