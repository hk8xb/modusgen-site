How many servers

Sized from your busiest hour, not your average, with the rest of each month left for batch work.

Servers = the busiest hour's output tokens a second ÷ (one server's speed × 0.67), rounded up.

Output is a small share of all tokens: about 0.6% for coding agents, 3% for employee agents, and 7 to 9% for chat and documents. For example, 200 heavy coding-agent users run about 190 billion tokens a month, and their busiest hour needs about 1,200 output tokens a second: two B300 servers at the faster speed, six at the slower. Overflow and failover go to a hosted endpoint.

A month covers the price written off over four years, power at $0.10 a kWh, colocation at $210 a kW-month and support at 5% of the price a year. After year four only power, colocation and support remain.
