Questions buyers ask first.

Are open-weight models good enough for our developers?

For part of the work, yes. They trail frontier models on the hardest tasks, so we test both on tasks from your own repositories. What passes moves; what does not stays on the model you use today.

Do we have to buy hardware?

No. The models can run in your own AWS or Azure account. If you want them on your own floor, we can also help you build airtight on-prem hosting.

What happens to our code and data?

It stays in your network. The deployment has no route to the internet, the gateway runs inside it, and it records token counts, never prompts or answers.

What exactly do you install?

Upstream open source: vLLM to serve the models, OpenCode as the coding agent, PostgreSQL, Prometheus and Grafana, Podman and Terraform, plus our own gateway and portal. OpenCode is used unmodified, and ModusGen is not affiliated with its maker.

Who runs it once it is live?

Your platform team, with our runbooks and about a day a week of their time during deployment. If you would rather we watched it, operations in business hours are an option.

How do you charge?

A fixed fee for each step, then a subscription once you are in production. We quote after the first call, when we have seen your numbers.

What if moving does not save us money?

Then the assessment says so in writing, including what you could save by switching models on your own. We would rather lose a project than sign you up for one that does not pay.
