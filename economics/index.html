At 2.0× the tokens, the hosted route costs 12% to 108% of the frontier price. At the top of that range, moving does not pay.

Frontier runs from Claude Haiku 5.5, at its price for the long prompts coding agents send, to Claude Opus 5.5. Your own GPUs are priced at public list prices, fully busy; half-idle machines cost twice as much per task.

Which route fits

Your own machines
When the data must stay inside, or the load is large and steady enough to keep the GPUs busy.
Your cloud account
When the data must stay private and the load is still settling. It also covers the wait for machines.
A hosted endpoint
When the data may leave your network. It is often the cheapest place to start, and we say so.
