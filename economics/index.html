Token for token, the open model of the same tier costs 63% of Opus 5.5's price per task, and 39% of Haiku 5.5's.

Prices read November 12, 2026. Kimi K3.1 Pro is level with Opus on terminal-agent tests and behind it on some broader ones; it charges the same for cached input, which is most of what agents read, so the top tier saves less than the small one. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash scores above Haiku, priced here for the long prompts coding agents send. No open-weight model reaches Sonnet 5.5, or Claude Fable 5.2 at $9.84 a task.

Which route fits

Your own machines
When the data must stay inside, or the load is large and steady enough to keep the GPUs busy.
Your cloud account
When the data must stay private and the load is still settling. It also covers the wait for machines.
A hosted endpoint
When the data may leave your network. It is often the cheapest place to start, and we say so.
